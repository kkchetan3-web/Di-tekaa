## Character sheet — PRAMOD KAKA (parody of Dr. Pramod Sawant)

### A. Identity (verified public record)
- **Dr. Pramod Pandurang Sawant**, b. 24 Apr 1973, Bicholim, Goa. 13th & current (also re-elected 14th) Chief Minister of Goa; assumed office 19 Mar 2019 after the death of Manohar Parrikar. BJP MLA for **Sanquelim (Sakhali)** since 2012. **Ayurvedic medical practitioner** (Ganga Ayurvedic Medical College, Kolhapur); MSW (Tilak Maharashtra Vidyapeeth). Former Speaker of the Goa Assembly; former chairman, Goa State Infrastructure Development Corporation. Spouse: Sulakshana Sawant.
- Sources: official site `drpramodsawant.in` (/journey), Simple Wikipedia, Oneindia politician profile, Hindustan Times / Firstpost / ANI / India Today coverage (see `docs/reference/` files captured).

### B. Visual characteristics (from public photographs)
- Medium complexion, round-ish face, clean-shaven, **short black hair with a side part**.
- **Rimless/thin-frame eyeglasses** — constant accessory in press-conference photos.
- Typical clothing: **white kurta + sleeveless Nehru/bandi jacket** (grey, cream or beige variants); formal bandhgala for events; sandals.
- Accessories: gold ring (right hand), wristwatch; frequently holds **papers** while speaking.
- Public expressions: earnest mid-speech open-mouth look; composed press-conference expression; occasional broad smile.
- References captured in `docs/reference/pramod-sawant-*.jpg|webp` (Indian Express 2022; Frontline/The Hindu 2024–25; drpramodsawant.in).

### C. Movement & body language (from public speech/press footage)
- Signature **pointing index-finger gesture** while making a point at the podium (visible in captured press-conference photos).
- Upright, measured posture; both hands on paper/lectern when not gesturing; measured head turns across the room while speaking.
- Distinctly **unhurried, deliberate delivery** — pace stays level even in rebuttals.

### D. Voice characteristics (public speeches; observed)
- Mid-register, soft-spoken-to-firm; Konkani-accented English with measured rhythm; repeats key policy phrases.
- Publicly repeated themes (documented in ANI/India Today/TOI coverage): **"double-engine government"**, "people will give us a chance", "it is my job/duty as a party worker", infrastructure & welfare-scheme emphasis, "we have a stable government".
- **Game audio policy:** no politician recordings are used or cloned. All in-game lines are **original parody dialogue written for this game**, performed by a synthetic voice — labelled in-game as parody, not authentic quotations.

### E. Fighting-game personality (design fiction, not a claim about the real person)
- Archetype: **"The Stable Government"** — balanced, patient all-rounder; gains meter while defending (stable = hard to destabilise); special: **"DOUBLE ENGINE CHARGE"** — an unstoppable shoulder-charge with a two-stage saffron exhaust trail; heavy: podium-slap palm; taunt: adjusts glasses then points ("Ek point…!"); victory: namaste + wave; defeat: straightens papers and exits with dignity.

## Character sheet — RANYALO POR (community persona; creative interpretation)

### A. Identity
- In-game name only. No verified public figure. Documented as a persona in the original's own UI (CPU default fighter).

### B–C. Visual / movement design (creative, for this project)
- A wiry young Goan everyman brawler: dark curly hair, thin fierce eyebrows, gold chain, **red sleeveless shirt** (the classic Goan "ranyalo" hot-blooded look), knee shorts, chappals, a gamallo (towel) over one shoulder.
- Movement: bouncing fight-stance, loose shoulders, sudden darts; taunt = hip-wiggle taunt-dance; victory = spin + arm-flex; defeat = face-plant and towel-drop.

### D. Voice (design)
- Fast, high-energy Konkani-inflected street patter. Original parody lines only ("Di tekaaaa!" battle-cry).

### E. Fighting-game personality (design fiction)
- Archetype: **"The Brawler Lad"** — fast, slightly erratic rushdown: quick walks, fast punch chains, lower health; special: **"RANN-FURY SPIN"** — a spinning flail attack that travels forward; taunt: wiggle-dance that mocks the opponent's health.

## Research integrity notes

- Verified facts (offices, profession, appearance) are separated from **creative interpretation** (all personalities, animations, movesets, dialogue — design fiction for parody purposes only).
- No footage was downloaded from paywalled/DRM sources; all references are publicly published photos/articles.
- No politician's voice is cloned; no statement fabricated by us is presented as a genuine quotation anywhere in the game or docs.
